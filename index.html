Email on your own server, on every device you own.

KyPost sits in front of the mailbox you already have. It holds your credentials, sorts mail with a model that runs on your hardware, and gives each phone, laptop, and browser its own key. Pairing a device is one QR scan.

How it fits together

Your devices never learn your mailbox password. They talk to KyPost, and KyPost talks to your provider. Each device hangs off its own arm with its own credential, so you can revoke one without touching the rest.

What stays where

The point of running your own server is knowing exactly which machine holds what.

On your server
Mailbox credentials, filter rules, contacts, and the classifier that sorts your inbox. By default nothing about your mail leaves the server for a cloud model.
On each device
A credential minted for that device alone, kept in the platform keystore. Lose a phone, revoke the phone.
Only on enrolled devices
End-to-end encrypted mail is decrypted on the devices you enroll. The server and the push relay never hold the key.

Set up in three steps

Most of an afternoon goes to choosing options, not to installing. The quick start walks through every one.

  1. 1

    Install

    Run the server with Docker Compose, then point it at your IMAP and SMTP provider from the Config screen.

  2. 2

    Pair

    Open Security, then Devices, in the web app and scan the QR code from each native app. Each code expires after 90 seconds.

  3. 3

    Choose

    Turn on device encryption, multi-factor sign-in, local sorting, and push delivery when you want them, one at a time.
