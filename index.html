Why KyPost

Control without giving up convenience

Your server

Connect the mailbox you already use. Credentials, rules, contacts, and local classification stay on infrastructure you choose.

Your devices

Pair once with a QR code. Each device receives a separate credential that you can revoke without disrupting the others.

Your keys

End-to-end encrypted messages are decrypted on enrolled devices—not by the KyPost relay.
