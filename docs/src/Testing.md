## Testing the KyPost Mobile App

Before you begin, make sure you have the test account credentials and a phone
with the KyPost app installed.

If you need a login, please join the KyPost Beta Google Group at <https://groups.google.com/g/kypost_close_beta>.

### Pair the app

1. On a computer, open <https://demo.kypost.org/>.
2. Sign in with the provided account.
3. Open **Settings**, then select **Security**.
4. Enter your account password.
5. On the **Sign On** tab, select **Enable 2FA** and complete the setup.
6. On your phone, open the KyPost app and allow notifications when prompted.
7. On the KyPost site, open **Devices** and select **Pair a new device**.
8. On your phone, select **Scan QR code** and scan the code displayed on the
   site.
9. Confirm that the phone appears on the site and that the app displays the
   server and subscriber ID.

### Enroll the device for PGP email

1. Close and reopen the app.
2. Open **Settings** and select **Set Up** at the bottom to enroll the device
   for PGP email.
3. On the KyPost site, select **Done**, then select **Enroll**.
4. Unlock your PGP key with your password.
5. On your phone, enter the key and your account password.
6. Verify your identity on the phone.

The app is now ready for feature testing.
